Protection settings

Prototype for controlled testing. Keep the original research extension disabled while using Guard to avoid duplicate capture.

POST-body inspection may happen after the first send. Follow-up rules apply only to cross-site subresources; same-site submissions are preserved. Saving settings or Reset learned rules clears learned policy.

Popup checks are best effort and can interrupt legitimate sign-in or payment windows. Allow a trusted site below if needed. Page scripts can bypass the popup hook; Chrome network rules remain separate.

Small starter list: doubleclick.net, googlesyndication.com, googleadservices.com. Hand-maintained tracker targets, not a comprehensive malware feed. Rules include subdomains. Allowing a site disables popup checks and outgoing network rules for that site. Rules also cover its subdomains. Matches raw email and SHA-1/256/512 hex. URL protection uses browser rules before send; body observation may occur after send. Values and digests stay in this browser profile and are left out of evidence exports.

DataExodus service (this computer or Raspberry Pi)

Guard talks to http://127.0.0.1:8010. Run the service on this computer, or on the Raspberry Pi and connect with pi\connect-pi.ps1 (an SSH tunnel: the Pi's service is never exposed on your network). It explains evidence, checks URLs and opens quarantined links in a throwaway sandbox browser. The explanation is written by the service itself, on that machine.
